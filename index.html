Some friction lives deeper than the problem in front of you.

This work is for founders, families and leaders carrying something complex. You can have every resource, every advisor and every reason for things to work, and still feel a dissonance that strategy, effort and conversation don't reach. It shows up as a pattern that keeps returning, however many times it's addressed.

  • A marriage that keeps circling the same place.
  • A family whose generations pull in different directions.
  • A partnership that looks right on paper and feels wrong in the room.
  • A deal that stalls for reasons no one at the table can name.
  • An organization that keeps losing energy it can't account for.
  • A family enterprise or estate passing from one generation to the next.
  • A founder whose company has outgrown the relationships it started with.

These patterns are rarely about the surface issue. They come from the relationships underneath: between people, between generations, and across dimensions, from what we can see here all the way up to the blueprint of what each thing is meant to be.

The work

A more beautiful song.

Picture an orchestra coming together for the first time, each player with their own instrument and history, there to perform one symphony. If the instruments are out of tune, what comes out is cacophony. The work is bringing them into one song.

It begins with the vision: the thing that wants to come into being. The vision tells you which strands belong in it, the people, relationships, places and needs that make up the whole. Each strand is first tuned within itself, so its own best version can come through. Then the strands are tuned to each other, around the vision. Within, then between.

Harmonizing isn't matching. No one gives themselves up to fit someone else. Each strand stays itself, and that is what lets it add to the song instead of disappearing into it. Sometimes the highest good is a harmonious parting, and the work serves that too.

The full name is transfrequential multidimensional potentiation, harmonization and amplification: holding many frequencies at once, across every dimension, from the densest to the lightest.

“We're all dropping stones into the same water. The work is learning what to do when the ripples meet.”

The method

Tune in. Potentiate. Harmonize. Amplify.

  1. I

    Tune in

    To the vision, and to every strand it holds.

  2. II

    Potentiate

    Clearing each strand, so its own best version can come through. Nothing is thrown away. What was dense is lightened until it can flow.

  3. III

    Harmonize

    Bringing the strands into one song around the vision, without anyone losing themselves.

  4. IV

    Amplify

    Letting energy run through what's been made, until it has the momentum to carry itself.
